About

Estimated fetal weight percentile

Hadlock FP, Harrist RB, Martinez-Poyer J. In utero analysis of fetal growth: a sonographic weight standard. Radiology 1991;181:129–133.

Median weight from the paper's equation, ln(weight) = 0.578 + 0.332 GA − 0.00354 GA² (GA in weeks). Source range: 10 to 41 weeks.

Umbilical artery S/D percentile

Acharya G, Wilsgaard T, Berntsen GK, Maltau JM, Kiserud T. Reference ranges for serial measurements of umbilical artery Doppler indices in the second half of pregnancy. Am J Obstet Gynecol 2005;192:937–944.

Measured at the free loop of the cord. Percentile from the paper's appendix formula for ln(S/D). Source range: 19 to 42 weeks.

Gestational age from an EDD

ACOG Committee Opinion No. 700: Methods for Estimating the Due Date. Obstet Gynecol 2017;129:e150–e154. The EDD is 280 days after the first day of the LMP, so GA on a given date is 280 days minus the days remaining to the EDD.

Limits

Both reference populations were singleton pregnancies. Outside a source's range of weeks the app still shows a value, marked Extrapolated.

Every coefficient was taken from the source paper and the app's output was checked against each paper's published tables; the comparison is in VALIDATION.md in the project's repository.

Nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere. The app works offline once opened.
